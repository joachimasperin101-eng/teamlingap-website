GENESIS BATCH

The Beginning of Service

The members of Team Lingap are the heart of the team. They are the ones who actively serve, inspire others, and carry the mission of compassion, faith, and charity in everything they do.



To create a stronger sense of belonging and identity, members are grouped into batches with their own special names. These names reflect the spirit, personality, and legacy of each generation.



Batch Genesis represents the pioneers — the very first members who planted the seeds of service, leadership, unity, and volunteerism within Team Lingap.
