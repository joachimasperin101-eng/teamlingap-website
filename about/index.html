About Team Lingap

A student-volunteer organization built on compassion, faith, service, and servant-leadership across generations.

Who We Are

Team Lingap Generations is a group of student-volunteers whose goal is to serve the institution and the community with compassion, faith, and charity.

The team supports school activities, joins outreach programs, and executes projects that benefit the wider community.

Guided by servant-leadership, initiative, and shared responsibility, Team Lingap believes that true impact begins from the heart.

Brief History

From small acts to lasting service

February 2024 — Humble Beginnings

Team Lingap began with a small group of seven volunteer students who offered their time, effort, and abilities in service to both the school and the community.

Foundation Anniversary Outreach

The group became united during the Foundation Anniversary Week of SBCA, where they voluntarily helped spearhead the Lingap Bernadettian Outreach Program.

Growing in Servant-Leadership

With the guidance of their moderator and founding members, the organization strengthened its direction and opened opportunities for students to grow as servant-leaders.

Team Lingap Generations

As members graduated and new students joined, the group embraced the identity “Team Lingap Generations,” representing the passing of service from one batch to another.

Service Beyond the School

The team continued to serve through outreach programs, school events, parish involvement, and community-based activities, reaching beyond the school walls.

About the Logo

The Team Lingap Generations logo reflects compassion, unity, diversity, and continuity.

The heart represents love, mercy, and compassion. The hand symbolizes teamwork and camaraderie. The different human figures show harmony despite differences.

The word “Generations” represents continuity, reminding members that the mission of service is passed from one batch to the next.

Vision and Mission

Guided by service across generations

Vision

We envision a community where people of all generations are united in compassion, empowered in leadership, and committed to sustainable acts of service that transform lives.

Mission

To embody the spirit of Lingap by extending genuine care, compassion, and assistance to individuals and communities in need, while fostering a culture of volunteerism and ensuring that the mission of service continues across generations.

Core Values

Values that guide every act of service

Respect

Honor and value every person encountered, creating a supportive environment where everyone feels seen, heard, and valued.

Responsibility

Take accountability for actions, decisions, and commitments while making choices that contribute to the well-being of the community.

Service to Humanity

Serve with genuine care and a willing heart, sharing time, talents, and resources to bring hope and positive change.

Love for God

Let faith guide every thought, word, and action, serving others with humility, compassion, integrity, and sincerity.

Objectives

Serving with purpose

Institutional Support

Actively participate in school activities and contribute to the success of institutional events.

Faith and Charity

Foster the spirit of service, faith, and charity toward the institution and wider community.

Youth Empowerment

Empower students through collaborative projects and acts of service for the common good.

Community Outreach

Engage in meaningful outreach and community projects that promote empathy and solidarity.

Leadership Growth

Help members become responsible, values-driven, and socially aware servant-leaders.

Positive Impact

Reach out to local communities, partner organizations, and institutions to make meaningful contributions.

“God doesn’t call the qualified, He qualifies the called.”

This motto reminds Team Lingap that service is not defined by prior experience, but by willingness, sincerity, faith, and commitment to grow through service.
