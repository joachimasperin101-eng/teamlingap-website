About the Program

Education support for children who need it most.

Education is one of the strongest foundations for a child’s future. Through this outreach program, Lingap aims to equip underprivileged school children in Alabang, Muntinlupa with essential school supplies and learning support.

The initiative also brings together businesses, organizations, and compassionate individuals who believe that every child deserves the opportunity to learn, dream, and succeed.

Program Activities

More than giving supplies

The outreach program combines material support, encouragement, and meaningful community engagement.

🎒

School Supply Distribution

Bags, notebooks, paper supplies, pencils, uniforms, shoes, art materials, and hygiene kits.

🍽️

Feeding Program

Snacks, bottled water, and meals may be provided to support the children during the outreach activity.

🌟

Inspirational Activities

Talks, games, volunteer engagement, and educational activities designed to uplift and encourage children.
